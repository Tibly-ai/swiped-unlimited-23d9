What you get

Built for a room that never stops moving

Every venue has its own rhythm: a 60-seat dining room, a service bar three deep on a Friday, a club with roaming staff. We study how your floor actually runs before we recommend hardware or a rate structure.

01 / Tableside

Checkout at the table

Handheld EMV chip and contactless terminals let servers close a check where the guest is sitting. Tips, splits and the card all stay at the table, so nobody walks a card back to a station.

02 / On the floor

Tap anywhere in the venue

Phone taps, watch taps, contactless cards. Bar service, bottle service, coat check and door cover all accept the same way, with no line forming around a single register.

03 / POS

Works with your POS

We are integrated into most major POS systems. We do not push one brand. After hundreds of installs across industries we know which systems owners keep and which ones they complain about, and we tell you honestly.

04 / Insights

Spending patterns you can use

05 / Cost control

Compliant dual pricing

Our surcharge and dual pricing programs are built to state and federal guidelines, so you can show a cash price and a card price without risking penalties. The Zero Fee Program takes card processing cost to 0%.

Easier to start than Square or Toast

We work the other direction: we fit our processing into the system you already use, and a person from our team does the configuration with you on the phone.
