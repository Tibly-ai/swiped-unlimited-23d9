Setup

Easier to switch than most people expect.

With Square or Toast, changing how you take payments usually means changing your whole register: new hardware, a rebuilt menu or item library, retrained staff, and a contract that decides what you can plug in next. We work the other way around. Swiped Unlimited is integrated into most major POS systems, so in many cases your existing setup stays exactly where it is and only the processing moves.

  1. 01

    We study the business first

    A short conversation about your ticket sizes, card mix, POS system and where payments slow you down. No cookie-cutter quote before we understand the operation.

  2. 02

    One application, one account

    You fill out a single merchant application. We handle underwriting, the pricing structure and, if you want it, the Zero Fee Program paperwork at the same time.

  3. 03

    We connect to your POS

    Terminals arrive preconfigured for your account. Integrations to major POS systems are set up on our end, so your item library, staff logins and reporting stay put.

  4. 04

    Go live with a person on the phone

    We walk your team through the first batch, the first refund and the first settlement report. After that you still call the same number and reach the same family-owned company.
