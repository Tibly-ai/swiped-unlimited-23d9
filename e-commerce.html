Setup

Faster to switch than the boxed processors.

Square and Toast expect your store to move onto their hardware, their cart and their rules. We work the other way around. You keep the platform you built, we integrate underneath it, and a person from New Jersey walks the setup with you instead of a ticket queue.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you sell and where

    A short call covers your cart, ticket sizes, chargeback history and how orders reach you. We study the business before we quote it, which is why the first proposal usually fits.

  2. 02

    We map it to your existing stack

    Hosted page, API or both. If you already run a POS, we are integrated with most major systems, so the online and in-store sides report together rather than fighting each other.

  3. 03

    Application, then the connection

    Paperwork is short and we fill in the parts we can. Your developer gets the integration details; if you do not have one, we set the hosted page up for you and send the link.

  4. 04

    Test, go live, keep our number

    We run test transactions with you before the switch. After launch you call the same people who set it up, not a general support line that has never seen your account.
