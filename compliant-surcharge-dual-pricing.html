What the program covers

Built compliant, then built around your counter

Every piece below ships as part of the setup. Nothing is a paid add-on, and nothing is left for you to research on a Sunday night.

01

State and federal alignment

Your program is configured against the rules that apply where you operate, including the states that restrict surcharging outright. If surcharge is not permitted for you, we run dual pricing instead.

02

Card brand rule handling

Credit only, never debit. Rate caps respected. The service charge itemized as its own line on the receipt, exactly the way the networks require it.

03

Disclosure signage and receipts

Entry and register signage plus the correct receipt language come with the program. Your staff gets a one page script so the first customer question has a clear answer.

04

Zero Fee Program

The end result for most merchants: 0% credit card fees. The cost of card acceptance moves to the card price, and your cash customers keep paying what they always paid.

05

Runs on your current POS

Swiped Unlimited is integrated with most major POS systems, plus smart terminals, virtual terminals, hosted payment pages and API invoicing. Dual pricing follows the transaction wherever it starts.

06

Customer trust stays intact

Posted pricing, clear signage and an itemized receipt. Customers see the same number at the shelf, at the register and on the statement, which is what keeps complaints and chargebacks down.

Setup path

Easier to switch than you were told

Signing up with Square or Toast usually means adopting their whole stack: their hardware, their menu builder, their reporting, their app store, and a rebuild of how your team rings up a sale. Changing pricing structure later means learning a second system on top of the first.

We do it the other way around. We come to the system you already use, configure the program inside it, and leave your workflow where your staff found it. One application, one review of your current statement, and a real person in New Jersey who answers the phone during setup.

See POS compatibility
  1. 01

    Send us one recent statement

    We read the effective rate, the card mix and the fees buried under the summary page. You get a plain breakdown of what you are paying now and what the program changes.

  2. 02

    Pick surcharge or dual pricing

    Surcharge adds a disclosed service charge on credit. Dual pricing posts a cash price and a card price. Your state rules, your ticket sizes and your customer base decide which one fits, and we walk you through both.

  3. 03

    We configure the hardware and the software

    EMV chip and contactless terminals arrive programmed. Existing POS installs are configured in place. Online and invoice payments get the same pricing logic through our hosted pages and API.

  4. 04

    Signage up, staff briefed

    Compliant disclosure signage ships with the equipment. Training takes about fifteen minutes because the register flow your team already knows stays the same.

  5. 05

    Go live, then keep watch

    Rules change. We monitor the program against current state and card brand requirements and adjust your configuration so it stays compliant after the day it launched.
